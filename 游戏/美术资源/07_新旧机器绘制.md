## 最终提示词

### 旧机

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: one standalone transparent PNG game prop for a sparse educational interactive about a factory. Draw an OLD MANUALLY DRIVEN MACHINE, a squat simple workshop press on a narrow workbench with two sturdy legs, a very conspicuous exposed large flywheel with THREE broad spokes and hand crank on the left, simple open vertical press linkage on the right. Use Nicky Case The Evolution of Trust visual language: very simple flat 2D marker drawing, slightly wobbly thick charcoal outlines, solid white and medium gray fills, minimal details, naive playful hand drawn silhouette. Not a realistic industrial illustration. Front/side orthographic view, no isometric perspective. One whole machine centered with safe margins in a wide landscape canvas roughly 2:1. This must read clearly at 240 pixels wide. Genuinely transparent alpha background, no floor, no shadow, no people, no products, no lettering or numbers, no labels, no AI symbols, no rust, no gradients, no texture speckles. Only the machine. The hand crank and exposed wheel are its key recognizable silhouette.

### 新机（最终采用）

Use case: illustration-story. Generate ONE new original machine sprite. A simple electric workshop press: squat wide enclosed gray housing, small motor cover on top with two vent lines, a tiny white panel with three black circular buttons, an open press throat, and a long WHITE conveyor belt extending to the right with two visible black-ring rollers. Two squat feet. Very sparse Nicky Case The Evolution of Trust style: thick slightly wobbly charcoal hand-marker outlines, plain gray and white fills, simple naive 2D side view. Entire object centered on a 2:1 landscape canvas with margins. Background must be genuinely transparent alpha. Isolated cutout. No floor or shadow or scene, no checkerboard drawn in image, no texture or gradient, no letters or numbers, no people or products, no exposed flywheel, no hand crank, no AI or robots. Readable silhouette as a small 240px educational game prop. Output a transparent PNG sprite.
