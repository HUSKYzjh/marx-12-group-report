This character-resource selection excludes third-party audio, libraries,
the laptop photo, weapons, blood and gore. Reference JS is preserved unchanged
for study; it is NOT executed or bundled into our game. It can refer to sounds
and scene assets not included here. Those references do not import those assets
or extend CC0 to them. Original PNGs/JSON atlases are unchanged. Per-file source
URLs, pinned Git blob hashes, dimensions and SHA-256 are in provenance.json.

The character-lab preview uses new Canvas crop/render/control code and adapts
original frame ranges and motion patterns. It is not the original game engine,
not a complete recreation of the original action timing, and not an official
Nicky Case project. Preview durations and motion intensity are our parameters.
Frame-based sequences are distinguished from procedural movement in motions.mjs.
No camera/news-cycle simulation, violence, audio or original story is executed.

Trust assets displayed alongside these are the pre-existing original assets
from ncase/trust at commit 6ec45d73befdb922bd40654dd1c1c903a953562f,
under its original CC0 dedication. The crazy laugh is face frame 10 plus hopping,
not the dizzy-face sequence 18–22 and not WBWWB's shouting CrazyPeep.

Character shape, clothing and expression do not assign moral value or nationality.
The full character-lab motion library remains a separate resource preview.
The bakery introduction now reuses hatguy as its owner and body/face as its driver and baker;
these are new narrative roles, not the original characters' moral attributes.
The chef hat, oven, truck and bread are newly authored local SVGs, not WBWWB assets.
Only the hat is added to the baker's unchanged original head, body and face.
The earlier full-body baker.svg is retained but no longer loaded by the game.
Our 16-second loading/payment/wage sequence does not run original WBWWB scripts.
