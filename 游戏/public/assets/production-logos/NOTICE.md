# Production-network logo sources and notices

Checked: 2026-10-03. SVG paths are locally embedded in the production extra, catalog, industry and AI symbol modules; no third-party image requests occur at runtime.

Twenty-nine of the thirty-five logo path definitions come from **Simple Icons 16.33.0**; three come from official websites and three from pinned Lobe Icons. The display wrapper removes the upstream title, makes the SVG decorative (`aria-hidden="true"`), and supplies one neutral fill, `#555`. Accessible names and official destination links are supplied by the surrounding page. The aspect ratio and path geometry are preserved. Per-asset sources are recorded below and in the corresponding source modules.

Simple Icons repository license: [CC0 1.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/16.33.0/LICENSE.md). Its [disclaimer](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/16.33.0/DISCLAIMER.md) distinguishes the repository license from individual icon rights and brand guidelines. **CC0 does not license trademark rights.** A missing individual license entry is not a statement that an asset is unrestricted.
